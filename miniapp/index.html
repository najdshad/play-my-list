Getting your music together…

Your music, in order. Made for Telegram

A GOOD PLACE TO START

From vault to playlist.

Your audio stays in Telegram. We just keep it in order.

  1. Bring your music.

    Send /setvault to the bot, then forward a message from your vault channel. The bot will index its audio.

  2. Find your flow.

    Create a playlist, find tracks in your vault, and arrange them just the way you like.

  3. Send it out.

    Add the bot as a channel admin with Post Messages and Delete Messages permissions, choose that channel, and publish.

Publishing replaces the bot’s previous posts. If Telegram cannot delete older posts, remove them manually and retry.
